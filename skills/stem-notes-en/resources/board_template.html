High-Frequency Pitfalls & Q&AReview
Q: Why is the external load RL in parallel with RC in AC analysis?
A: In the AC equivalent circuit, DC supply VCC acts as AC ground and coupling capacitor C2 acts as a short, placing RC and RL in parallel from collector to ground.
